SyntaxCompiler Annotations

Planned annotations in spec:

  • @integer(bitcount, signed)
  • @float(bitcount (or standard?))
  • @size(bytecount)
  • @allign(bytecount)
  • @throwable
  • @iterable - for use in for (T val : iterable_object)
  • @volatile - don't reorder my insides for optimizations, because I have a reason not to want that
  • @mutates - I mutate the "this" argument. Don't let me be called for a constant. (Replace with keyword? probably)

Unknown annotations should be treated as if they don't exist by the compiler.
